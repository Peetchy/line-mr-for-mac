LINE MR: VoiceOver support for LINE

LINE MR makes LINE for Mac work with VoiceOver: buttons get names, and the chat list, messages and call window can be read and used from the keyboard.

This installer puts LINE MR in your Applications folder. It does not change LINE itself.

You need:

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or later
  • LINE from the Mac App Store (tested with LINE 26.5.0)

LINE MR is an unofficial project and is not affiliated with LY Corporation.
